# Movents user guide

## Sign in and set up your society
Sign in with Google or your email (we send a one-time link). The first time, create your society and pick your
university, or join a society you were invited to. Presidents and VPs invite the committee from
**Settings → Members** with a role and department. Switch between societies from your name at the bottom-left.

## Connect your AI assistant
**Settings → Connect your AI assistant** shows the server URL and lets you create tokens. Claude, ChatGPT and other
apps with a "Connect" button only need the URL. See [AI_ASSISTANTS.md](AI_ASSISTANTS.md).

## Plan an event (Committee member)
1. Ask your AI assistant, e.g. *Plan a 150-pax AI talk on 15 Nov, budget RM3,000*, or use **New event** in the app
   and type the same line.
2. Watch the live trace. Green = done, amber = adapted or warning, lock = blocked by your role.
3. Open outputs from the right-hand panel: proposal, budget sheet, risk register, run sheet, calendar.
4. Anything outbound you can't send is now in **Approvals** and the approver has been notified.

## Approve (President / VP / Director)
**Approvals** → read the preview → **Approve & send** (or Reject with a note). Directors only see requests for their
own department. The faculty submission emails the advisor a signed approve/reject link.

## Run the event
- **Event → Budget**: type actual spend as receipts arrive. At 90% of the approved budget the President is alerted.
- **Event → Speakers & vendors**: mark replies, confirm speakers (President), rate vendors, pay deposits.
- **Event → Registration**: publish to Luma/Meetup, sync RSVPs, see predicted attendance and how much food to order,
  promote the waitlist. Share the public page/QR.
- **Event → Event day**: 15-minute run sheet (click to edit), roster, emergency contacts.
- **Check-in** (phone): type or scan ticket QR codes at the door. Install the app to your home screen for full screen.

## After the event
Run *Generate the post-event report for the AI talk.* The report covers registered vs actual, budget vs actual,
cost per attendee, NPS, feedback themes and lessons learned. Lessons go into the SOP knowledge base and the next
plan applies them automatically (e.g. the show rate used to size food).

## SOP knowledge base (President / VP)
**SOP knowledge base** → upload a .md/.txt SOP or paste it. Headings like *Proposal format*, *Approval chain*, *Lead
times*, *Rules* and lines like *Venue booking: at least 6 weeks before* are detected and applied.
New university? **Universities → +** (add it with its SOP and venues).

## Automations (daily)
Speaker follow-up after 3 days without reply · vendor reminders 7 days before · attendee reminders 6w/4w/2w/3d/day-of ·
90% spend alert · feedback survey the day after. Presidents and VPs can run them now from **Settings**.

## Audit log
Every sign-in, agent step, approval, email, payment and decision — who, what, when, approved by. Filter by event,
switch to table view, export CSV.
