# Dormathon 2026 submission

Movents started as a Dormathon 2026 entry (Track 2, Tencent WorkBuddy) by team **Moomin Valley** from Taylor's
University: Teo Zen Ben, Jeanette Tan En Jie and Colin Leong Hong Seng.

The hackathon build ran in a demo mode with seeded societies and an account switcher. That mode was replaced by real
accounts, society workspaces and support for any MCP-capable AI assistant.

For the Google Cloud AI Builder Cup 2026 the app was rebuilt around Gemini on Google Cloud: an in-app team of Gemini
agents on Vertex AI, deployed on Cloud Run, with a "Try the live demo" sandbox for judges. See the
[README](../README.md).
